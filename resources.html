What to Check Before a Claim

A cashless facility does not necessarily mean that every hospital charge will be paid by the insurer. Reviewing the policy beforehand can help you understand the possible payment gap.

  • Check the sum insured and applicable policy limits.
  • Review room-rent and ICU restrictions.
  • Look for disease-specific or treatment-specific sub-limits.
  • Understand co-payment and deductible provisions.
  • Review exclusions and waiting periods.
  • Understand how non-payable hospital expenses may affect the final bill.
  • Keep important policy and medical documents accessible.

Understanding Cashless Claims

Cashless treatment generally involves communication between the hospital and insurer or claims administrator. Approval of a claim does not automatically mean that the entire hospital bill will be paid.

  • Cashless approval may be subject to policy terms.
  • The approved amount may differ from the hospital's final bill.
  • Non-covered expenses may remain payable by the policyholder.
  • Policy limits can affect the amount finally payable.

Understanding Out-of-Pocket Costs

The amount paid by the policyholder can depend on several factors, not simply on the total amount of the hospital bill.

  • Policy exclusions
  • Room-rent restrictions
  • Sub-limits
  • Co-payment
  • Deductibles
  • Non-payable or excluded expenses
  • Charges above applicable policy limits
