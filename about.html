ABOUT THE LAB

Understanding Health Insurance Beyond the Policy Brochure.

Health Insurance Behaviour Lab is an educational platform focused on how health insurance actually works at the time of a claim.

What This Lab Does

This website explains practical health insurance issues in simple language — especially the areas that can affect what a policyholder finally pays during hospitalisation.

The focus is on claims, cashless treatment, policy limits, exclusions, room-rent restrictions, package pricing and out-of-pocket costs.

Why This Matters

A policy may show a large sum insured, yet the amount payable for a particular hospital bill can depend on many policy terms and claim-level factors.

The purpose of this Lab is to make those mechanisms easier to understand before and during a claim.

Practical Experience Behind the Analysis

The analysis on this website is informed by more than 25 years of practical experience in health insurance.

Claims Behaviour

How claim approval, deductions, non-payable items and hospital billing can interact.

Policy Design

How limits, sub-limits, exclusions, room-rent conditions and other policy structures can affect claims.

Consumer Understanding

Clear explanations designed to help readers ask better questions and understand their policy documents.

What This Website Is Not

This is an educational and analytical website. It does not sell insurance policies, provide individual insurance recommendations, or guarantee claim outcomes.

Policy terms and claim decisions can vary by insurer, policy wording, hospital billing and the facts of an individual case.
