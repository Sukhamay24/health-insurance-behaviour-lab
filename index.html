Understanding Health Insurance Beyond the Policy Brochure

A health insurance policy may look straightforward when you buy it. The real experience can be very different when a hospital bill arrives.

A cashless claim may be approved, yet the patient may still have to pay a significant amount from their own pocket.

The difference can come from policy conditions, exclusions, sub-limits, room-rent restrictions, non-payable items, package pricing and other aspects of claim settlement.

Health Insurance Behaviour Lab explains these issues in simple language, using practical examples and real-world claim situations.

“My Claim Was Approved. Why Did I Still Have to Pay?”

This is one of the most confusing experiences for health insurance policyholders.

Claim approval and full payment of the hospital bill are not necessarily the same thing. Depending on the policy and the hospital bill, some amounts may remain payable by the patient.

Understanding what the policy covers, what it limits and what it excludes can make the claim process easier to understand.
